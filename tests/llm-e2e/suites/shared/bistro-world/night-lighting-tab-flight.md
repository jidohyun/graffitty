# Bistro night lighting and session-flight landings

## Open the baked Bistro

Open nine shell sessions and select the installed `bistro` world. Use the actual
application, with its default terminal effects and saved per-session framing.

## Inspect every destination

Click sessions 1 through 9 in order. At each stop, inspect the ground, walls, and
furniture/foliage, especially sessions 7–9. Fixtures should illuminate nearby
surfaces; merely increasing exposure or a uniform fill does not prove this.
Unlit streets must retain a dark night appearance, with warm local pools of
light; daylight-like blanket fill fails. There should be no giant glowing rectangles, clipped scenery, or black voids.
Save scenery-only and full-window captures, recording any remaining defects.

## Watch continuous flights

Record the actual session clicks and the entire motion. Include the street/cafe
entrance, the outdoor destinations, and the long return from 9 to 1. Examine
frames through the last quarter and the final landing. The camera should slow
gradually, without a late sideways flick, abrupt turn, stop-and-restart, or cut.
Check actual displayed position, orientation, and lens as supporting evidence;
a smooth endpoint or a passing scripted check alone is insufficient.

## Interrupt and revisit

Click a different session during a flight, then let it land. Pinch one session to 1.2x,
visit another, and return. Both the interruption and return should be continuous,
with the saved framing restored. Report PASS, FAIL, or BLOCKED with the recording,
lighting captures, and frame/route telemetry. A rendered capture is required for
lighting; do not claim a headless run establishes visual quality.

## Resize during a flight

Start another session flight and resize the application to a taller aspect ratio
while it moves. The camera must keep moving continuously and land at the new
framing, without snapping to a different lens at the end. Record the resize and
at least one second after arrival.
